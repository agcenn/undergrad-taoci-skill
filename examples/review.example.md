# Review Example

## Before

> 老师您好，我一直非常仰慕您在人工智能领域取得的卓越成果，也拜读了您的很多论文。我拥有丰富的人工智能科研经验，希望加入您的实验室学习。

## Problems

1. No professor-specific research anchor.
2. “拜读很多论文” is unsupported unless the student confirms it.
3. “丰富科研经验” may exaggerate an undergraduate background.
4. The message could be sent unchanged to many professors.

## Better direction

Use one verified current research topic, connect it to one real student project, and state the missing expertise as a learning goal rather than existing mastery.
