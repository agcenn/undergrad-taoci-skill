# Email Writing

## Structure

### Subject

Make identity + purpose visible.

Chinese examples:
- 西交计算机本科生申请参与课题组科研学习
- 本科生科研申请｜[方向]｜[学校/姓名]

English examples:
- Undergraduate Research Inquiry — [Area] — [Name]
- XJTU Undergraduate Interested in [Research Area]

### Paragraph 1 — Who you are

Use only essential facts:
- university / major / year
- one strongest relevant fact if useful

Do not lead with a long awards list.

### Paragraph 2 — Why this mentor

Mention one specific, verified research anchor. Show understanding in one or two sentences.

Do not write:
- “久仰大名”
- “拜读了您所有论文”
- “您在该领域取得了卓越成就”
- formulaic flattery

### Paragraph 3 — Why you may contribute / learn effectively

Connect 1–2 real experiences to the research anchor.

For undergraduates, valid evidence includes:
- course projects
- open-source work
- GitHub implementations
- competitions
- lab exposure
- systems/engineering skills

### Paragraph 4 — Request and commitment

State what you hope to do. Mention weekly time / duration only if known.

### Close

Mention attached CV only if there is actually a CV. Keep the closing simple.

## Length

Default:
- Chinese: around 220–350 Chinese characters in the body
- English: around 150–220 words

## Tone

- polite
- specific
- calm
- undergraduate-appropriate
- confident without pretending expertise

## Paper mentions

Only mention a paper title when it materially strengthens the connection and the student can plausibly discuss it if asked.

If the system researched the paper but the user has not personally read it, write about the **mentor's research topic**, not “I carefully read your paper”.
