# Follow-up

Use only after an initial outreach email has already been sent and the user wants to follow up.

## Default principle

One brief, low-pressure follow-up is usually enough. Do not create urgency or guilt.

## Content

- greet the professor
- identify the previous email briefly
- restate the research interest in one line
- politely ask whether there may be an opportunity to discuss
- keep it shorter than the original email

Do not copy the original email again.

## Timing

If the user asks about timing, consider academic context, weekends/holidays, and the professor's likely workload. If current dates matter, verify them rather than guessing.
